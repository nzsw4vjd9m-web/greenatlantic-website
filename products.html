Sealed edge trim

Our sealed edge process leaves a clean, smooth edge on every board instead of an open cut face. The edge resists dirt on the jobsite, wipes down years later, and gives you a square reference on every rip. All trim ships in 18’ lengths.

Sheets

From 1mm to 1 1/4”, in 10’ and 20’ standard lengths. The 20’ sheet takes board and batten from foundation to roofline with no horizontal joint. Consistent density makes them predictable to mill, rout and fabricate, which is why they show up in signage, cabinet shops, boat work and store fixtures as often as on houses.

Corners

Folded corner boards are precision-folded and glued from a single sheet, so there is no outside seam to split or stain. Extruded outside corners carry an integrated nailing flange and siding pocket and go on in one pass. Our 45° inside and outside corners arrive as one piece at the correct angle for bay windows, breakfast nooks and bump-outs.

Custom sizes

If the stock list does not match the job, we can produce the width, length, thickness or profile you need. Minimum order quantities may apply. Sign makers, cabinet shops, boat builders and store fixture companies all run Green Atlantic, and we are set up to supply large volumes of a single custom size.

What to send us. A dimensioned sketch or CAD file, the quantity and the length. To match an existing profile, a photo of the end with a tape in frame is usually enough to quote from.
