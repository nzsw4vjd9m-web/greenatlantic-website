Performance

Flame spread index below 200 when tested to ASTM E84.

Deemed equivalent to preservative-treated or naturally durable wood for resistance to weathering and termite attack.

Not affected by corrosion from contact with ACQ-D treated wood.

Soffit assemblies of 1/2” x 11 1/4” boards on framing at 16” on center carry an allowable design load of 25 psf negative wind pressure.

Approved uses

Corner boards, soffits, fascia, battens, door pilasters, frieze boards, nonload-bearing rake boards, architectural millwork, and door and window trim on buildings of combustible, nonfire-resistance rated construction.

Fastening

Stainless steel or hot-dipped galvanized fasteners designed for wood trim and siding, with blunt points and full round heads, long enough to penetrate the wood substrate at least 1 1/2”. Trim installs over solid backing; fascia over an approved structural sub-fascia.

Installation must follow the research report, our published installation instructions and the applicable code. Where they conflict, the report governs. Final approval rests with the building official in your jurisdiction.

Working with the material.

PVC is not wood and it is not fiber cement. A few habits make the difference between a job that looks right for twenty years and one that telegraphs every joint by the second summer.

Cutting and milling

Standard carbide woodworking tools. Run a slower feed rate than in wood to keep the cut clean. Our boards can be milled, routed and shaped on site.

Expansion and contraction

PVC moves with temperature, far more along its length than across its width. Gap butt joints and long runs to suit the temperature on the day you install, and fasten from the center of the board outward.

Fastening

Fasten close to the edge without predrilling. Use two fasteners per framing member on wide boards.

Painting

Paint is optional; our white needs no finish. If you paint, use 100% acrylic latex rated for exterior use and follow the paint maker’s guidance on light reflective value for darker colors.

Adhesive and joints

Use a PVC-specific cement at scarf and miter joints, and fill nail holes with a product made for PVC rather than wood filler.

Handling and storage

Keep bundles flat and supported, out of prolonged direct sun, and pull the protective film before or right after the board goes up.
