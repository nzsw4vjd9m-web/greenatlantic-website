About the manufacturer.

Green Atlantic PVC is made in a facility that has produced premium PVC and vinyl products since 2007. The Green Atlantic brand launched in 2018 to put that capability behind a trim line built for exterior contractors.

Quality at the front of the line.

Quality control sits at the start of the process rather than the end of it. Automated feed hoppers keep density consistent through every run, and our edge-sealing system finishes every board the same way, on every length and in every width.

Boards leave the line wrapped in our own protective film, so they arrive free of dents and scratches with the edges sealed.

Sold direct through one step.

Most premium trim passes through two or three hands before it reaches a jobsite, and every one of them prices it. We sell through one-step distributors only. Same board, fewer margins stacked on top of it, which is how a premium board lands on the job at a number that still works on a competitive bid.
